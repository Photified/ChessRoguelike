How to Play

Goal: Eliminate all enemies to clear the wave. Do not get captured.

  • Enemies: Pawns march forward. If they reach the bottom, they instantly promote to Queens.
  • Smart AI: Enemies will actively try to dodge your attacks and set up traps.
  • Drafting: Clear waves to draft game-breaking perks. At levels 3 and 6, your piece evolves.
  • Scoring: Earn massive points by clearing levels using as few turns as possible.
